# Prompt externo — built-in image generation

Use case: compositing / identity-preserve automotive product photography.
Asset type: 4:3 approval preview for a luxury car dealership.
Image 1 is the only authority for the exact Porsche Panamera: preserve its exact
camera angle, viewpoint, silhouette, wheel orientation, proportions, ride
height, paint identity, wheels, lime-green brake calipers, lights, glass,
badges, trim, panel gaps, exhausts, spoiler, plate-cover dimensions and every
vehicle detail. Do not borrow vehicle pose or perspective from Image 2.
Image 2 is authority only for the exact Garage 665 Studio G: continuous plain
off-white ceiling without a visible luminous panel, off-white cyclorama wall,
light-gray floor, physical matte wall sign with four standoffs, 5200 K neutral
color, large soft upper/front-left key, right fill two stops lower, grounded
tire shadow and moderate photographic contrast.
Place the exact car from Image 1 physically inside the exact studio from Image
2. Replace literal outdoor reflections with broad softbox reflections while
keeping the original dark graphite metallic base, fine flake and polished
clear-coat. Never matte, satin, wrapped, uniform or CGI. Keep the exact source
viewpoint. No halo, pasted look, changed wheels, lights, badges, plate or glass.

# Prompt interno — built-in image generation

Use case: precise-object-edit / identity-preserve interior automotive
photography. Image 1 is absolute documentary authority for camera, crop, focal
length, dashboard, steering wheel, gauges, numbers, warning icons, screens,
buttons, console, seats, stitching, leather, trim, mirrors and pillars. Image 2
is authority only for the exterior Studio G and its 5200 K soft light. Make the
interior look captured while the car is parked in that studio. Through windows,
show only physically plausible distant off-white studio surfaces with natural
depth of field. In tight steering-wheel views, do not show the sign, full room,
corners, ceiling or cyclorama line. Preserve every interior value and detail.
No street, sky, logo inside the cabin, opaque windows or render-like materials.
